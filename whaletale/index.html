WhaleTale

A company in Baku that builds biometric, security, transport and infrastructure systems, from the circuit board and the enclosure to the software and the web panel that runs them. I co-founded it, work on the electrical engineering, and I'm responsible for most of the newer products on this page.

1 E-Gate

Automated border control. A traveller puts a passport on the reader, the gate reads the chip over RFID/NFC, checks the document under ultraviolet and infrared light, matches the face and fingerprints, and opens the turnstile if everything agrees. Incidents go straight to an operator. It is built for airports, sea and road checkpoints and railway stations.

PartWhat it does
document readerreads the passport chip over RFID/NFC and checks the page under UV and IR light
biometricsface and fingerprint match with anti-spoofing
turnstileopens on a match, with an emergency unlock

2 Think-ID

A portable enrolment terminal for identity documents. It takes a face photo, all ten fingerprints with a multispectral scanner, and a signature with its stroke dynamics, then checks quality on the device before anything is sent.

3 Smart Bus

A city bus built around one onboard computer that ties together GPS, LiDAR and radar, outside cameras, passenger information panels, stop announcements, card readers and turnstiles that count passengers. The cameras feed driver assistance: collision warning, lane keeping and adaptive cruise control.

4 Flight Controller

A flight controller board for drones, designed in-house: processor, gyroscopes, accelerometers, magnetometer and GPS on one PCB in a sealed housing. It flies manual or fully autonomous, returns to base, lands on its own when something fails, and logs telemetry for later.

5 E-Padlock

A steel padlock for cargo that only opens with an encrypted NFC card. It tracks its position by GPS, falls back to satellite where there is no other signal, and uses vibration, impact and motion sensors to report tampering to a monitoring panel.

SpecValue
batterylithium, up to 2 years

6 Residence Manager

Access and billing for apartment buildings. A lift card reader that only goes to your own floor, running on a custom board with in-house microcontroller firmware; an entrance intercom with video calls to the resident's phone; gates that open from the app or by reading the number plate; and one bill for all of it.

7 Fingerprint Controller

A fingerprint terminal that records attendance for people serving community sentences. It checks finger temperature and tissue to reject fake prints, pairs the print with an RFID card, keeps working offline, and reports to a central dashboard.

8 E-Bracelets

Electronic ankle monitors. Optical and capacitive sensors catch removal attempts, a pulse and temperature sensor confirms someone is wearing it, and it reports either to a base station over RF or on its own over GPS and GSM.

9 Smart Glasses

Glasses for people with low vision that read printed text aloud. A camera captures the page, the text is recognised, and it is spoken through the speaker, with voice control for speed and volume. Shown at the opening of an ASAN xidmət branch in 2021.
