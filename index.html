1 Work

  • Full Heartsweb · TypeScript, Next.js · 2026

    A Minecraft modpack builder that resolves dependencies, matches versions to the loader and checks the set against live Modrinth and CurseForge data before exporting one .mrpack. open · source

  • The Quiet Courierweb · Python, TypeScript, Next.js, Postgres · 2026

    A daily newspaper for Kindle and Kobo. One finished edition arrives at 5 a.m., laid out like an early-1900s broadsheet, and then it ends. It only uses licensed sources and carries no ads. sample · source

  • ForsakeAIbrowser · TypeScript, Svelte, WebGPU · 2026

    Marks the phrases that make text read as machine-written and rewrites them with a small model on your own graphics card. Every change can be undone, and numbers, names and links are left alone. open · source

  • lumenLinux · HolyC, OpenGL 3.3, GLSL · 2026

    A HolyC host for real-time fragment shader scenes, with an HDR bloom chain, hot reload and a deterministic offline renderer. Like Ossuary and the HolyC Graphics Guide, it is HolyC, Terry Davis's language, compiled to a native binary with hcc instead of running inside TempleOS. So far it has only been tested on Linux. notes · source

  • OssuaryLinux · HolyC, SDL2, OpenGL · 2026

    A first-person catacomb horror prototype. Find five relics, open the gate, get out before the warden finds you. Your lantern lets you see, and lets it see you. One level, no audio yet. download · docs · source

  • ESP-NOW Auto-PairingESP32 · Arduino C++ · 2025

    Two ESP32 boards find each other over ESP-NOW on first boot by broadcasting, then save the partner's MAC address to EEPROM so they reconnect straight away after a restart. If sends keep failing, the board clears the pairing and starts over. source

  • PowerGuard360hackathon · TypeScript, Python, React Native, Arduino · 2026

    Predictive maintenance for a gas turbine plant, built by a team at CodeRED (University of Houston) for the Chevron track. A live sensor board feeds one turbine, two Random Forest models say which unit needs maintenance and why, the manager follows the plant in 3D, and engineers get the job on their phones. I built the plant server, the website and the phone app, and helped Anthony get his sensor board talking to the server. Like most hackathon code, a lot of it was written with AI help. demo video · source

2 WhaleTale

WhaleTale designs biometric, security, transport and infrastructure systems in Azerbaijan, from the PCB to deployment. I'm a co-founder and work on the electrical engineering. Some of what the company makes:

  • E-Gate, automated border control with biometrics and document checks
  • Think-ID, a mobile terminal for enrolling face, fingerprints and signature
  • Smart Bus and Bus Manager, a connected city bus and its media and passenger-count panels
  • Flight Controller, a flight controller board with its own sensors and autopilot
  • E-Padlock, a GPS cargo padlock opened by an encrypted NFC card
  • E-Bracelets, Fingerprint Controller, Smart Glasses and Residence Manager

Photos and details for each product are in my notes on WhaleTale and on whaletale.tech.
